## Platform

web

## Users
- **Primary Users**: Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs/Pymes) in Colombia and Hispanoamérica, along with their commercial/sales directors. They need to sell to the state (their country's largest client) but lack legal training.
- **Secondary Users**: Independent professionals (consultants, accountants, engineers, lawyers) looking to acquire expertise in public procurement to offer bidding services or advance their careers.

## Product Purpose
Avantia is a practical online academy and consulting service for public procurement and bidding. It exists to democratize and simplify access to government contracts, allowing businesses and professionals to successfully submit competitive, error-free bids and win public contracts.

## Positioning
Avantia delivers 100% practical, zero-jargon training and support led exclusively by active, practicing procurement consultants who structure and win multi-million dollar contracts weekly. It differs from traditional legal/academic programs by focusing on step-by-step portal execution (e.g., SECOP II) and immediate business results.

## Operating Context
- Navigating official public procurement portals, primarily Colombia's SECOP II and other Latin American bidding systems.
- Analyzing complex bid documents (pliegos de condiciones), identifying pitfalls, structuring technical and financial proposals, resolving observations, and managing the post-closing process through to final contract award.

## Product Principles
1. **Practicality Over Theory**: Strip out unnecessary legal/technical jargon. Focus on real pliegos de condiciones and hands-on portal usage from day one.
2. **Active Consultant Guidance**: Ensure all instruction and mentoring comes from professionals actively winning bids in the current market.
3. **Downloadable Readiness**: Equip learners with immediately reusable templates, models, and checklists to streamline their actual bidding process.
4. **End-to-End Execution**: Cover the complete process from filtering opportunities and preparing the offer to responding to observations and final audience defense.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Clear typography hierarchy and contrast-compliant colors suitable for business professionals and team members of varying technical/visual abilities.
